About Gigga

Empowering South African students through flexible work opportunities

Our Mission

Gigga was founded with a simple goal: to bridge the gap between talented South African students seeking flexible work opportunities and local businesses looking for skilled, motivated talent. We believe every student deserves the chance to gain real-world experience, build their portfolio, and earn income while pursuing their education.
